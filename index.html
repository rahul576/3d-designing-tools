How it works

01

Pick a tool

Choose the generator that matches what you want to make, from the tabs above.

02

Set your numbers

Drag, type or click. The 3D preview updates live, with weight, cost and print-time estimates.

03

Download and print

Export STL or 3MF, drop it into your slicer and hit print. Settings are remembered for next time.
