3MF Viewer

Open a .3mf or .stl file with the Open button, or drop it on the page.
  • Colours come from the file: painted triangles, per-part filaments and colour groups, the way Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer save them.
  • The model sits on a 10 mm build-plate grid, centred like on a slicer plate. All plates and objects in the file are shown.
  • Untick a colour on the left to hide those parts and look inside.
  • PNG saves the current view as a picture.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The file is read on your computer.
Mouse: drag to turn, right-drag or Shift-drag to move, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. Touch: one finger turns, two fingers pinch and move.
